## What is public here

[harness-audit](https://github.com/fabianmartinelli-fm/harness-audit) audits the harness of an AI coding project (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Antigravity, with or without Obsidian), restructures it, and measures how much of your instruction files the agents were actually reading, before and after.

It exists because I kept finding agents on my own repositories reading a fraction of the AGENTS.md I had written for them. One pilot came back at ten percent.

Most of what I build is client work and stays private. When a piece is general enough to stand on its own, it ends up here.
